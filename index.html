01 — Research

Small organisms.
Extraordinary influence.

01

Marine & coastal fungi

Documenting the diversity and ecology of fungi in saltmarshes, intertidal habitats, seagrass beds, forests, and other coastal environments.

02

Molecular biology

Using molecular tools to investigate fungal identity, gene expression, evolution, and interactions.

03

Genomics & metagenomics

Applying genome sequencing and environmental DNA approaches to explore fungal diversity and community function.

04

Coastal plant-fungal interactions

Investigating fungi associated with coastal and marine plants, including their ecological roles and host interactions.

05

Fungal biodiversity

Documenting and comparing fungal diversity across habitats to reveal patterns, distribution, and overlooked species.

06

Fungi in agroecosystems

Studying fungal communities and their roles across agricultural landscapes, soils, crops, and managed ecosystems.

07

Arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi

Investigating AMF diversity, root associations, nutrient exchange, and contributions to plant health and soil function.

08

Lichens

Exploring lichen diversity, ecology, symbiosis, and responses to environmental change across coastal and forest habitats.

09

Endophytes

Characterizing fungal endophytes in plants and investigating their ecology, diversity, and potential biological roles.

10

Fungi in biocontrol

Assessing fungal organisms and interactions for sustainable biological control in agriculture and natural systems.

11

Bioremediation

Investigating fungal capabilities to transform, break down, and reduce environmental contaminants.
